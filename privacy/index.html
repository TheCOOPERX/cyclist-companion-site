Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Cyclist Companion collects nothing.

No account. No sign-up. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. The app makes no network requests at all, so there is no server for your data to go to — because there is no server.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — your bikes, components, tyre and wheel choices, saved pressures and fit records — is written to your device's own local storage by the operating system. It never leaves the device unless you choose to export it.

Because the data is local, uninstalling the app deletes it. If you want to keep it, export a backup first (see below).

What we never see

  • Your identity. There is no account and no login, so the app has no idea who you are.
  • Your location. The app requests no location permission and does not use GPS.
  • Your usage. There is no analytics SDK. We do not know which screens you open, how often you use the app, or whether you use it at all.
  • Your data. Your bikes, weights, measurements and setups stay on your device.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. The app requests none of these permissions.

Backups you create yourself

The Backup screen exports your data as a JSON file. On a phone this opens the system share sheet; on the web it downloads the file. You choose where it goes — iCloud, Google Drive, Dropbox, email, or nowhere at all.

Once you hand that file to another service, that service's own privacy policy governs it. We are not party to it and never receive a copy.

Third-party code

The app is built with Flutter and uses a small number of open-source packages to talk to the operating system: local storage, the share sheet, the file picker and temporary file paths. None of them are advertising or analytics SDKs, and none of them transmit data off the device.

What the App Store sees

Apple and Google operate the stores the app is distributed through, and they collect their own information about downloads and crashes under their own policies. If you have opted in to sharing analytics with Apple, we may see anonymous, aggregated crash and download statistics through App Store Connect. That data comes from Apple, not from the app, and it cannot identify you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is nothing to collect and no way to submit it.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. Your data is already entirely in your hands: it is on your device, you can export it at any time, and deleting the app erases it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that needs the network — a weather lookup for temperature-adjusted pressure is the most likely candidate — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. We will not start collecting data quietly.
