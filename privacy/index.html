Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Cyclist Companion collects nothing.

No account. No sign-up. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. We run no server of our own and receive no riding data. Native platform services, including the rating prompt, may communicate with Apple.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — your optional chosen name, rider measurements, unit preferences, colour theme, preferred bike shops, bikes, components, tyre and wheel choices, saved pressures, fit records, suspension settings and maintenance tasks — is written to your device's own local storage by the operating system. We do not receive it. Your device backup settings may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup, under that service's privacy policy. You can also choose to export a backup yourself.

Because the data is local, uninstalling the app deletes it. If you want to keep it, export a backup first (see below).

Optional maintenance reminders

On iPhone, you can explicitly enable on-device maintenance notifications. iOS asks for notification permission only when you enable them. Reminders are scheduled locally, not sent by a server. The notification uses generic wording and does not include your task title, bike name, notes or measurements. The permission preference and scheduling history stay on this installation and are not included in exported rider backups. You can turn reminders off in the app or iOS Settings; the in-app calendar remains available without permission.

What we never see

  • Your chosen name. There is no account or sign-in. You may optionally enter a name for your Home greeting. The name you choose is stored on your device alongside your rider preferences and is included in backups you export. We have no server of our own and receive none of this information. You control where exported files are saved or shared and your device-level backup settings.
  • Your location. The app requests no location permission and does not use GPS.
  • Your usage. There is no analytics SDK. We do not know which screens you open, how often you use the app, or whether you use it at all.
  • Your data. Your bikes, weights, measurements and setups are stored locally unless you choose to export them or include them in a device backup.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. The app requests none of these permissions.

Backups you create yourself

The Backup screen exports your data as a JSON file. On a phone this opens the system share sheet; on the web it downloads the file. You choose where it goes — iCloud, Google Drive, Dropbox, email, or nowhere at all.

Once you hand that file to another service, that service's own privacy policy governs it. We are not party to it and never receive a copy.

Free update and earlier versions

Version 1.1 makes every feature free and removes in-app purchases. This update is being prepared for release. Upgrading keeps your riding data; no purchase or purchase restoration is needed to use its features.

Earlier versions offered an optional one-time Pro unlock through Apple. Historical transactions remain subject to Apple\'s privacy policy.

The app may also ask iOS to show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt after you have used it a few times. That prompt is drawn by iOS, not by us: we are not told whether it appeared, what you rated, or whether you wrote anything.

Third-party code

The app uses Flutter and open-source packages for local storage, the share sheet, file selection, temporary paths and the native rating prompt. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs. Version 1.1 removes the purchase package; native platform services may still communicate with Apple.

What the App Store sees

Apple and Google operate the stores the app is distributed through, and they collect their own information about downloads and crashes under their own policies. If you have opted in to sharing analytics with Apple, we may see anonymous, aggregated crash and download statistics through App Store Connect. That data comes from Apple, not from the app, and it cannot identify you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is nothing to collect and no way to submit it.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. Your data is already entirely in your hands: it is on your device, you can export it at any time, and deleting the app erases it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that needs the network — a weather lookup for temperature-adjusted pressure is the most likely candidate — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. We will not start collecting data quietly.
