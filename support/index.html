Getting started

How do I get a tyre pressure recommendation?

Open the tyre pressure screen, pick your tyre's brand, model and width, and enter your system weight — that is you, plus the bike, plus anything you're carrying, in your riding kit. Then set the surface and riding style. Front and rear are calculated separately.

If you've added the bike to your Stable, you can select it and the tyre details fill in automatically.

What is "system weight"?

The total mass the tyres carry: rider in full kit, bike, bottles, tools, bags and their contents. It is not your body weight alone. Getting this right matters more than any other input — a 10 kg error moves the recommendation by several psi.

What does the weight distribution slider do?

It sets how much of the system weight sits on the front wheel, which is why the front and rear recommendations differ. A road bike carries roughly 45–48% on the front; a time trial or track bike more; a mountain bike less. If you don't know yours, leave it at the default.

My tyre isn't in the list.

Pick any tyre of the same width and type — the calculation uses the width, the casing type and your conditions, not the specific model, so the result will be the same. You can also type a width in directly.

Then email us the make and model and it will go into the next update. This is the most useful thing you can send us.

My groupset isn't in the list.

You can type chainrings and cassette cogs in directly as comma-separated numbers — for example 50,34 and 11,12,13,14,15,17,19,21,23,25,28,32 — and everything works the same way.

As with tyres, tell us what's missing and it goes in the next update.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and nowhere else. The app has no account and no server to send anything to. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Backup & Restore and tap Export backup. Save the JSON file somewhere you can reach from the new phone — iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or email it to yourself. On the new phone, install the app, open Backup & Restore and tap Import backup.

Importing replaces everything currently in the app, so export from the new phone first if it already has data you want to keep.

I deleted something by accident.

There is no undo yet. If you have a recent backup, importing it will bring the data back — but it replaces everything, so you would lose anything added since that backup. Exporting a backup occasionally is worth the ten seconds.

Does the app work offline?

Entirely. It has no online mode to lose. Aeroplanes, alpine passes and hotel wifi make no difference to it.

Known limits

Fat bikes and very wide tyres

The pressure model is accurate for tyres up to roughly 65 mm. Above that it over-estimates, so fat-bike riders should treat the number as an upper bound and go by feel. Widening this range is on the list.

Why is my recommendation capped?

Tubeless setups are capped at 72 psi, the usual ceiling for hookless tubeless rims. When a cap is applied the app tells you what the uncapped figure would have been, so you can decide with your rim and tyre manufacturer's limits in front of you.

Is this a replacement for a professional bike fit?

No. The fit journal is a notebook: it records the measurements you take and shows you what changed between two sessions. It does not tell you what your position should be, and it is not a substitute for a fitter.

Accuracy

Pressure recommendations are starting points, not guarantees. Tyres, rims and riders vary. Always verify with an accurate gauge, and never exceed the maximum pressure printed on your tyre or specified by your rim manufacturer — particularly with hookless rims, where over-inflation can unseat a tyre.
